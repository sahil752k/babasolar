About Baba Solar

Powering India’s Clean Energy Future With Engineering, Innovation & Trust

Baba Solar is one of Maharashtra’s fastest-growing premium solar companies delivering high-performance residential and commercial solar systems.

Our Story

Founded with a mission to simplify solar adoption in India, Baba Solar has grown into a trusted renewable-energy brand known for engineering precision, transparency and customer satisfaction.

2018 — The Beginning

Started with small residential rooftop installations and a strong vision for quality.

2020 — Commercial Expansion

Entered large commercial and industrial solar projects across Maharashtra.

2023 — Premium Brand Positioning

Focused on advanced monitoring, premium components and modern design.

2025 — Franchise & Growth

Launched Baba Solar Franchise Program for rapid expansion.

What Makes Baba Solar Different

Premium Components

Engineering Excellence

Systems designed specifically for Indian climate and grid conditions.

Customer-First Service

Support from consultation, installation to long-term maintenance.
