About Baba Solar

Powering India’s Clean Energy Future With Engineering, Innovation & Trust

Baba Solar is one of Maharashtra’s fastest-growing premium solar companies, delivering high-performance rooftop and commercial solar systems. With a commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction, we transform homes and businesses into energy-independent spaces.

Our Story

Founded with the mission to simplify solar adoption in India, Baba Solar has grown into a trusted renewable energy brand known for its engineering excellence and customer-first approach. What started as a small 3-member team has now become a full-scale solar technology company serving thousands of customers across Maharashtra.

2018 — The Beginning

Started with small residential projects and a vision to modernize solar installation standards in India.

2020 — Commercial Expansion

Enabled large industrial and commercial solar setups with strong technical partnerships.

2023 — Premium Brand Positioning

Shifted towards high-quality design, advanced monitoring, and safety-driven engineering processes.

2025 — Franchise & Growth

Launched Baba Solar Franchise Program — helping entrepreneurs build profitable solar businesses.
